# Echelon Commercial public content style guide

## Editorial rule

Do not use the em dash character in visible copy, headings, labels, descriptions, captions, or newly created design text. Use a period, colon, comma, parentheses, or a deliberate line break instead.

## Design intent

Copy should read as concise, specific, and commercially useful. Avoid filler phrases, generic claims, repetitive sentence patterns, and punctuation that makes the writing feel machine-generated. Visuals should support the audience named by the section and should use clear hierarchy, balanced spacing, natural contrast, and purposeful calls to action.

## Scope

This guide applies to the public portfolio documentation and to future storefront updates. It does not change product facts, approved specifications, legal wording, or source-document content unless those materials are separately reviewed and approved.
